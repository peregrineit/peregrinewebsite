- The copyright policy and the data feed services policy are optional for each local MLS. The policy statement on
  the use of MLS data is not labeled optional by NAR; Real Estate News reports it as mandatory. The committee
  qualifications concern NAR's own committee.
- No effective dates are stated, because NAR has published none. No MLS is named as having adopted anything.
- NAR's documents do not mention AI, and the guide does not say the policies address it.
- The approval sequence differs between NAR and Real Estate News, so the guide says "approved at NAR NXT and
  announced on November 8, 2026" and reports both accounts.
